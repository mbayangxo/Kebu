# Kebu — Global Access Philosophy

**Core line:** **Build for everyone. Empower Africans with more.**

**Agent rule:** `.cursor/rules/kebu-global-access.mdc`

---

## What Kebu is doing

Kebu is **not** trying to lock technology away from the world.

Kebu is building **technology infrastructure from an African perspective** — while giving Africans an **additional layer** specifically designed to **close the opportunity gap**.

That is not exclusion. It is **empowerment on top of open infrastructure**.

---

## The clean boundary

| Layer | Who | What |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Global Kebu** | **Everyone** | Account, Search, Builder, Shop, Cloud, Mail, Domains, Analytics, Business tools, Reach, Studio — when each module is live |
| **African empowerment layer** | **Verified Africans** (entitlement) | Kebu Opportunity OS protected intelligence, deeper trade/resource/program matching, eligibility-gated economic intelligence |

**Public may discover** Opportunity OS exists. **Protected data** checks entitlement server-side — not scattered `if African` checks in UI.

**Personal eligibility ≠ Kebu ID.** A business identity is not proof of personal eligibility for protected intelligence.

---

## Engineering rules

1. **Do not** build an “Africans-only internet.”  
2. **Do not** hide Builder, Search, Mail, or Cloud from global users when those products are live.  
3. **Do not** hard-code ethnicity/country checks across the codebase — use **centralized entitlements** (e.g. `african_opportunity_access`).  
4. **Do** verify once → persist entitlement on account → check server-side on protected routes.  
5. **Do** label protected vs public data with trust labels (Verified / Estimated / AI-generated / Requires validation).  
6. **Kebu Search stays open globally** — separate from entitlement-gated Opportunity OS intelligence.

---

## Product framing (for Cursor and humans)

**Wrong:** “Kebu is only for Africans.”  
**Wrong:** “Kebu locks the best tools behind verification.”  

**Right:** “Kebu is African-built infrastructure **for the world** — with an **extra** opportunity layer for Africans who verify, because closing the opportunity gap requires depth global platforms do not provide.”

**Marketing:** Everything you need to build your business — **for anyone**.  
**Mission:** Give African youth **more** — not less access for everyone else.
