# Kebu — No watching (brand principle)

**Agent rule:** `.cursor/rules/kebu-no-watching.mdc`

---

## The principle

**No watching** is a **Kebu brand principle**, not a marketing tagline.

Young people do not learn to build businesses or software by bingeing lecture videos. They learn by **actually building** — and getting unstuck inside the same tools they use to launch.

**Wrong framing:**

> “Learn to code” = traditional programming course · video modules · LMS progress bars · certificates for watching

**Kebu framing:**

> **Learn by building** — open a real project, ship something, hit a wall, get guidance, ship again.

---

## What “learn to code” means on Kebu

When we say learn to code (or learn to build), we mean:

1. **Open a project** — website, store, app draft, opportunity research, business record  
2. **Ship something real** — publish, sell, register, submit  
3. **Get stuck** — honest errors, missing steps, real constraints  
4. **Get unstuck** — Yande / docs / templates / community **attached to the work**  
5. **Ship again** — iteration, not consumption  

Guidance lives **inside Builder, Shop, Studio (Teach me), Opportunity OS, Kebu ID, Cloud (when live)** — not in a separate “school” product unless explicitly assigned.

**Studio Teach me:** when a young person chooses Teach me on AI create, Kebu still builds an editable design and explains *why* (hierarchy, color, format…) on that project — learn by building, not by watching modules.

---

## Allowed in Phase One (without Kebu Learn product)

- Plain-language explanations in Builder and registration  
- Next-action UX (“do this now”)  
- Yande helping **on the actual site/store/project**  
- Opportunity OS research that leads to **Build This**  

---

## Forbidden until assigned

- Course catalog · lecture video LMS · “watch to unlock” progress theater  
- Fake “Learn” nav that is only YouTube embeds  
- Passive “education” disconnected from Kebu projects  

**Kebu Learn** (future product) must inherit **No watching** — project-based paths only.

---

## Brand voice

Use **No watching** where it fits product copy — especially youth-facing Builder, Learn-adjacent flows, and Yande Code positioning:

- “We don’t teach you to watch. We help you build.”  
- “No watching. Real projects.”  

Do not sound childish. Stay serious enough for real businesses.
